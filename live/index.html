Before you go live

  • Open all five once on the demo machine so the fonts cache — they'll still render if the venue wifi drops mid-session.
  • Share the browser window, not the whole screen. Nothing from Follow Up Boss should ever be one alt-tab away.
  • Press F11 for fullscreen before you start sharing, not after.
  • Close every other tab. A notification preview on a client thread is the one mistake you can't take back.
